Farmer first

Life at CultAI

Work that reaches every acre.

Our teams sit at the meeting point of agriculture, technology, and global trade. One week you're in the field with soil data, the next you're mapping a new export corridor or shipping a model to production.

We're remote-friendly, collaborative, and deliberately cross-disciplinary — agronomists and engineers, traders and data scientists, all working on the same connected platform.
